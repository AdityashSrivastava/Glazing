# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A dedicated closed-loop squad of 5 high-performing engineers and students (`Adityash`, `Manas`, `Shivansh`, `Praveen`, `Harshit`). They need transparent, peer-driven daily accountability across technical studies, software engineering, and physical conditioning.

## Product Purpose

Glazing transforms daily execution into a structured, competitive habit through transparent squad telemetry, peer pressure, and verifiable proof-of-work. Success means consistently logging focused execution hours before the daily midnight IST cutoff without administrative overhead.

## Positioning

A closed-loop, peer-pressured productivity matrix combining hard deliverables with verifiable proof-of-work and social stakes (P2P point bounties and weekly sponsor penalties), anchored by a clean, subdued productivity aesthetic rather than loud, distracting gamification.

## Operating Context

- **Daily Ritual**: Tasks are initiated during the workday, tracked with integrated Pomodoro/stopwatch timers, completed with proof-of-work, and locked in before the strict 12:00 AM IST midnight freeze.
- **Weekly Sprint**: Runs Monday through Sunday 23:59:59 IST. The weekly champion earns bragging rights, while 4th and 5th place operatives fulfill the "Paneer Patties Party Protocol".
- **Environment**: Desktop web browser during work/study sessions, accessible via local Vite server or deployed Vercel/Render stack.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **5-Domain Competency Matrix**: Tasks are categorized across DSA (multiplier 15), Development (12.5), College Studies/Work (10), Gym Checkpoint (+5 pts/day), and Life (5 pts/hr).
- **Verifiable Proof of Work**: Completed tasks require proof links or uploaded screenshots with automated domain validation (+5 pts accuracy bonus).
- **P2P Staking & Bounties**: Operatives stake earned lifetime points on peers' active deliverables.
- **Privacy Mode**: "Classified Directives" allow masking sensitive task titles while keeping leaderboard verification intact.
- **Hard Constraints**: Closed-loop authentication strictly limited to the 5 designated operatives; no public registration.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Identity**: Sleek, minimal dark productivity tool. Subdued, purposeful gamification focused on quiet deep work and high scanability. No cartoonish or noisy over-stimulation.
- **Color Palette & Accents**: Jet black/zinc surface canvas, high-contrast crisp typography, subtle emerald conditioning accents, and warm amber sprint indicators.

## Evidence on Hand

- Fully functional Single Page Application (`/frontend`) with views for Activity Feed, Leaderboards, Macro Goals, Pomodoro Focus Timer, Competency Analytics, and Bounty Board.
- Live database schema supporting 5 operatives, persistent daily/weekly historical logs, and storage buckets for proof artifacts.

## Product Principles

1. **Subdued Focus Over Loud Noise**: The interface recedes during deep execution; telemetry and stats provide clarity without visual clutter or intrusive celebratory fluff.
2. **Transparent, Verifiable Execution**: Proof-of-work and time accuracy anchor the trust model; points cannot be claimed without verifiable evidence.
3. **Strict Midnight IST Cadence**: All sprints and daily counters enforce a firm Indian Standard Time midnight rhythm to cultivate shared squad tempo.
4. **Instant Telemetry**: Telemetry metrics, squad pulses, and radar competencies must load instantaneously without blocking operative workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigability across all modal dialogs, timers, and task initiation workflows.
- High contrast text on dark backgrounds conforming to WCAG AA guidelines.
- Semantic HTML and unambiguous status indicators that do not rely solely on color to convey state.
